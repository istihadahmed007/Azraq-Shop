# Archived Legacy Storefront Prototypes

These components represent initial exploratory design sections created during the early prototyping of the AZRAQ storefront. They are archived here to keep the active production component tree clean and maintainable.

## Archived Components
- **BestPicksSection.jsx**: Early 8-card product grid. Superseded by `TrendingRightNowSection.jsx` and `ProductGrid.jsx`.
- **DarkFeatureBar.jsx**: Early 4-pill feature strip with neon highlights. Superseded by `ProductDemonstrationSection.jsx` and the header/footer assurance strips.
- **EditorialSection.jsx**: Early editorial tastemaker quote section. Superseded by `EditorialCtaSection.jsx`.
- **FeaturedCollectionsCarousel.jsx**: Horizontal scroll carousel of featured categories. Superseded by `FeaturedCollectionsSection.jsx` (4-card editorial grid).
- **NewArrivalsSection.jsx**: Early prototype for new product launches. Superseded by `TrendingRightNowSection.jsx`.
- **ProductCarouselSection.jsx**: Early tabbed category carousel. Superseded by `ShopByCollectionSection.jsx` and `ProductGrid.jsx`.
- **TrendingDiscoveriesSection.jsx**: Early editor's picks layout. Superseded by `TrendingRightNowSection.jsx`.

> **Note:** Thin backward-compatible stubs remain in `src/components/` so any historical references continue to resolve without breaking the module graph.
